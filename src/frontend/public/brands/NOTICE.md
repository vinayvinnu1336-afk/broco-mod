# Brand logos

The SVG files in this folder come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) (npm package `simple-icons` v16.35.0),
which is released under the CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication.

All brand names, logos and trademarks are the property of their respective owners. BroCo uses them only to
identify the vehicle make a customer selects; their use does not imply any affiliation with or endorsement by
the trademark owners.

Files were generated with each icon's official Simple Icons path and brand colour (very light colours are
replaced with #0A192F so they stay visible on white). Brands without a Simple Icons logo have `"logo": ""`
in `vehicle-catalogue.json` and the app shows a letter tile instead.
