# Brand logos

All brand names, logos and trademarks are the property of their respective owners. BroCo uses them only to
identify the vehicle make a customer selects; their use does not imply any affiliation with or endorsement by
the trademark owners.

Brands without a logo file have `"logo": ""` in `vehicle-catalogue.json` and the app shows a letter tile instead.

## Retired brands

BYD, Daewoo and DC Design were removed from the catalogue, as were Tesla, VinFast and Jayem (electric cars only; BroCo does not service electric vehicles). Their logo files were deleted.
